A board game about the small decisions that build — or wreck — a bank account. Roll, land, choose. Ten rounds each. Highest net worth wins.

Everyone starts with RM2,000 cash and no debt. Landing on Bills Due you must pay, or the bank covers it as an emergency loan. Debt earns 8% interest every time you pass Start. Ten rounds — then it's final net worth (cash minus debt) that crowns the winner.
